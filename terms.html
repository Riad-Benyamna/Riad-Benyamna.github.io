What Kaky does

Kaky is a voice-first nutrition tracking app. It allows you to:

  • Describe meals using your voice or by typing
  • Receive an AI-generated estimate of each food's calories and macronutrients
  • Log meals and track daily totals against personal goals
  • Sync your data across devices when signed in

The app is provided free of charge. We reserve the right to introduce paid features in future versions; you will be notified before any payment is required.

AI features and accuracy

Kaky uses a third-party AI service (OpenRouter routing requests to Google's Gemini AI model) to identify foods from your descriptions and estimate their nutritional content. By using these features you acknowledge:

  • AI estimates are not exact. Calorie and macro values are approximations based on typical food data and may vary from the actual values of what you eat.
  • The AI may misinterpret ambiguous descriptions. You are responsible for reviewing and correcting results before saving.
  • Network conditions may affect AI response time or availability.
  • You must grant explicit consent before any data is shared with the AI service. You can revoke consent at any time from Settings.

Tip: When portion size matters, use the in-app portion controls (½×, 1×, 2×) to refine the estimate. The more specific your description, the more accurate the result.
