Good questions

Straightanswers.

Is typing really unlimited on the free plan?

Yes. Keyboard meal logging is unlimited. Free users also receive two voice logs every rolling 24-hour period.

How accurate are calorie estimates?

Kaky is designed to produce useful nutrition estimates from the details you provide, but food preparation and portions vary. Review the result before saving, and use an explicit calorie amount whenever you know it.

What happens to a voice recording?

Kaky records only when you start a voice log, trims silence, and securely sends the short clip for transcription and nutrition analysis. Read the Privacy Policy for the complete data flow.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes. Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are managed by Apple. Cancel in your Apple subscription settings before renewal; your access continues through the paid period.
