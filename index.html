Why kaky.

Logging food shouldn't feel like work.

We built kaky for the people who quit every other tracker by week two. Speak naturally, get accurate numbers, move on with your day.

Talk, don't type
Log a full meal in five seconds. Say "three eggs and toast" — kaky understands real-world portions.
Accurate by design
AI returns per-100g nutrition density; kaky computes the math using the same Atwater factors nutritionists use.
Fix portions instantly
One tap to halve, double, or refine any item. The numbers recalculate from the source — no compounding drift.
Daily macros, at a glance
Calorie ring, macro bars, streak counter. Everything you need on one screen — nothing you don't.
Offline-first
Log meals on the subway, on a flight, in the woods. Everything syncs the moment you're back online.
Privacy-first
Speech is transcribed on-device. Only the text is sent for nutrition analysis. Your meal data stays yours.
How it works

From spoken meal to logged macros — in seconds.

01
Tap and speak
Press the mic. Describe your meal the way you'd tell a friend — "two eggs, toast with butter, and a coffee with milk."
02
AI identifies each item
A nutrition-trained model breaks down every food, with per-100g density for protein, carbs, fat.
03
Refine in one tap
Portion off? Hit ½×, 1×, or 2×. Wrong food? Swap it. Kaky recalculates on the fly.
04
Save and move on
One tap saves the meal. Your daily ring updates. Total time: about five seconds.
