Good questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Write to hello@kaky.org.

Is typing really unlimited on the free plan?

Yes. Typed meal logging is unlimited. Free members also get two voice logs a day, and the count resets at midnight in your time zone.

How accurate are the estimates?

Kaky gives each food you name its own line and uses nutrition data for plain foods, but recipes and portions vary. Review the result before saving, and say the calories whenever you know them; Kaky keeps the number you give.

What does Kaky do with Apple Health?

Only what you turn on in Settings: it reads your weight into your progress and saves each meal's calories, protein, carbs and fat to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising.

What happens to a voice recording?

Kaky records only when you start a voice log, trims silence, and sends the short clip for transcription and nutrition analysis. The Privacy Policy describes the full data flow.

Do I need an account?

No. Start as a guest and protect your progress later with Apple or Google. Kaky does not sell your personal data.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Cancel any time in your Apple ID subscription settings, and restore purchases from the Kaky Pro screen or Settings.
