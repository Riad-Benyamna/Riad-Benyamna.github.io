The 20-second version

Kaky uses your information to run Kaky—not to advertise at you.

Meal text and short voice recordings are processed to provide the logging feature you request. Guest nutrition data can remain on your device; signing in enables supported cloud sync. Kaky does not sell personal data or use third-party advertising trackers.

01

Where this applies

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaky (“Kaky,” “we,” “us,” or “the app”), developed by Mira Acaya, handles information when you use the Kaky iOS application, its account and cloud-sync services, and the kaky.org website.

Kaky is a nutrition-tracking tool, not a medical provider or medical device. Nutrition, body-weight, and goal information may be sensitive, so we limit its use to operating, securing, and improving the features you choose to use.

03

What happens after you tap

Voice and AI processing

Kaky requests microphone permission only when you choose voice logging. While listening, Kaky displays a responsive waveform, detects when you finish speaking, and creates a short audio file. Leading and trailing silence are trimmed before upload.

Voice transcription is not performed solely on your iPhone. The trimmed recording is sent over an encrypted connection through Kaky’s Supabase function to OpenRouter and an AI transcription provider. The resulting meal text is then processed for nutrition analysis.

A service request may include the audio or meal text, food-logging instructions, and technical request metadata. It does not intentionally include your name or email. Kaky does not add the audio recording to your meal history or intentionally retain the recording after the transcription request is completed. Processing providers may handle request data under their own terms and privacy commitments.

AI-generated nutrition is an estimate. Review the item, serving, calories, and nutrients before saving. When you provide an explicit calorie amount, Kaky treats it as your supplied value instead of replacing it with an AI estimate.

You can decline microphone permission and continue using manual text logging. Permission can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings.

Purpose, not profiling

How we use data

  • Provide onboarding, nutrition targets, logging, history, water tracking, weight check-ins, and progress insights.
  • Transcribe voice logs and generate the nutrition result you request.

We do not sell personal data, use nutrition or voice data for third-party advertising, or allow data brokers to build profiles from it.

06

Local first, cloud when chosen

Storage, retention, and security

Guest data

Guest profile, onboarding, meal, water, weight, and settings data are stored in Kaky’s app storage on your device. Removing the app can delete this local data. A random anti-abuse installation identifier is stored in the iOS Keychain and may remain after reinstalling; Kaky’s server receives and stores only a cryptographic representation for voice-limit enforcement.

Signed-in data

When you sign in, supported profile and tracking data are synced to Supabase and associated with your account identifier. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and service providers apply their own controls to stored data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Account and synced tracking data are retained while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Security and voice-usage events are retained as needed to enforce allowances and investigate abuse. Apple, RevenueCat, and Kaky may retain purchase records for entitlement, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal obligations.

Deleting your Kaky account removes account data under our control, subject to limited backups, security records, transaction records, and legally required retention. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

07

Controls that stay yours

Your choices and rights

  • Guest mode: keep supported tracking data local without creating a cloud account.
  • Microphone: allow or revoke access in iOS Settings; manual entry remains available.
  • Notifications: allow or deny Apple’s permission dialog, change reminder preferences, or disable Kaky notifications.
  • Review and correct: edit food results, servings, profile values, water, and weight data.
  • Delete your account: use Settings → Delete account to request removal of the Kaky account and associated cloud data.
  • Access or export: contact us to request access to or a copy of personal data under our control.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel Kaky Pro through Apple’s subscription settings.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights such as access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.
