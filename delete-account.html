Account and data deletion

Delete your account.For real.

Kaky’s deletion control lives inside the app so only the signed-in account owner can authorize it. There is no pretend web form and no password sent by email.

See the steps I cannot access the app
Authenticated deletion

Your request is verified before anything is removed.

Kaky sends the deletion request using your active account session. If the server cannot confirm deletion, the app keeps your local data and tells you to retry.

No public form can delete someone else’s account.
Cloud data is removed before the device is cleared.
Apple subscriptions are handled separately by Apple.
Delete from your iPhone

Four clear steps.No waiting screen.

Use the same Apple or Google sign-in connected to your Kaky account, then delete directly from Settings.

STEP 01

Open Kaky and go to Settings.

From the Home or Insights screen, tap the settings control in the top area.

Kaky → Settings
STEP 02

Scroll to your account controls.

Signed-in members will see an Account section. Guest accounts will see a Data section.

Account or Data
STEP 03

Choose the red deletion action.

Tap “Delete Account.” For an anonymous guest account, tap “Delete Kaky data.”

Delete Account
STEP 04

Read the warning and confirm.

Kaky sends an authenticated deletion request. After the server confirms it, the app clears local Kaky data and signs you out.

Confirm deletion
What happens after confirmation

Server first.Then your device.

Kaky does not show a success state until its account-deletion service confirms that the account was removed.

01

Your identity is verified

The request uses the active Supabase account session. A public email address alone cannot authorize deletion.

02

Your Kaky account is removed

Deleting the authenticated user triggers removal of associated Kaky profile and tracking records under Kaky’s control.

03

Local Kaky data is cleared

Only after server success does the app clear its local cache, reset account state, and sign out on that device.

If deletion fails: Kaky does not pretend the account is gone. Your local data remains available, an error is shown, and you can retry or contact support.
Scope of deletion

What goes.What stays separate.

Account deletion removes Kaky data under our control. Some billing and security records follow separate rules.

Important for Kaky Pro

Deleting Kaky does not cancel Apple billing.

Apple controls App Store subscriptions. Cancel Kaky Pro separately if you do not want it to renew.

Manage Apple subscriptions
01

Account profile and sign-in

Your Kaky authentication account and cloud profile are removed.

02

Nutrition and progress records

Associated meal logs, goals, weight check-ins, hydration records, and account settings are removed.

03

Local app data

Kaky clears its local account cache and saved tracking data on the device after server confirmation.

04

Limited retained records

Apple transactions, provider records, short-lived security data, limited backups, or records legally required to be retained may remain as described in the Privacy Policy.
